# Sharing

Everything here but email is in Settings > Sharing.

## Published feed

Articles you publish ("Publish" on an article, or `S`) go into an RSS feed anyone with its address can subscribe to: your 50 newest published articles. "Feed address" shows it to copy; "Change address" makes a new one, and the old one stops working.

### Publish a page

"Publish a page" adds any web page to your published feed, whether or not you follow its site. Leave "Title" or "Text" empty to take them from the page. Publishing the same address again updates it.

### Bookmarklets

Drag "Publish in Newses" or "Subscribe in Newses" to your browser's bookmarks bar. On any page, they open Newses ready to publish that page (with any text you selected) or subscribe to its feed; nothing happens until you confirm.

[TTRSS apps](./clients) can share pages to your published feed too, and on Android, sharing a link to the Newses app offers to publish it.

## Share links

"Share" on an article creates a public link to it that anyone can open without signing in. It shows the article's title, feed, author, date, and content, with a link to the original; never your notes, labels, tags, or score. The link keeps working after the feed drops the article, until you choose "Stop sharing".

"Shared articles" lists every article you share, to open or stop sharing.

Shared pages ask search engines not to index them.

A shared page's own text, such as its date and its link to the original, is in the language of the visitor's browser when Newses has it, else your language, else English. The article stays as it was written. Your published feed's title and description are in your language.

## Email an article

"Email" on an article (or `a m`) offers two ways. "Send from Newses" sends it from your server to up to 5 addresses, with an optional note: the whole article, or a link with the start of the article. The mail comes from the server's address, names you as the sender, and replies go to your email address, so it needs an address you confirmed in Settings > Account ([Email address](./account#email-address)) and a server that can send email. With "Load article images through this server" on ([Images](./reading#images)), the article's images load through your Newses server. The mail's own text is in your language.

"Share from this device", in the same dialog, needs neither: it hands the article's title and web address to your device, along with its public link if you made one, and you pick who gets it there. In a browser that has a share sheet, and in the Android app, the share sheet opens; otherwise, and in the desktop app, a new mail opens in your mail app. An article without a web address needs a public link first. The dialog opens on the choice you made last on that device.
