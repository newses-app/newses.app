# Sharing

Everything here is in Settings > Sharing.

## Published feed

Articles you publish ("Publish" on an article, or `S`) go into an RSS feed anyone with its address can subscribe to: your 50 newest published articles. "Feed address" shows it to copy; "Change address" makes a new one, and the old one stops working.

### Publish a page

"Publish a page" adds any web page to your published feed, whether or not you follow its site. Leave "Title" or "Text" empty to take them from the page. Publishing the same address again updates it.

### Bookmarklets

Drag "Publish in Newses" or "Subscribe in Newses" to your browser's bookmarks bar. On any page, they open Newses ready to publish that page (with any text you selected) or subscribe to its feed; nothing happens until you confirm.

[TTRSS apps](./clients) can share pages to your published feed too, and on Android, sharing a link to the Newses app offers to publish it.

## Share links

"Share" on an article creates a public link to it that anyone can open without signing in. It shows the article's title, feed, author, date, and content, with a link to the original; never your notes, labels, tags, or score. The link keeps working after the feed drops the article, until you choose "Stop sharing".

"Shared articles" lists every article you share, to open or stop sharing.

Shared pages ask search engines not to index them.
